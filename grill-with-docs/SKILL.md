# Grill with docs

Load and follow `grilling` for the interview and `domain-modeling` for the repository's glossary and
ADRs. Run them as one design loop: resolve the design-tree frontier with the owner while recording
terms as they settle and offering an ADR when a decision meets the domain-modeling threshold.

Complete when the grilling frontier is empty, the owner confirms shared understanding, every resolved
term is reflected in the applicable `CONTEXT.md`, and every accepted ADR is written at the repository's
established location.
